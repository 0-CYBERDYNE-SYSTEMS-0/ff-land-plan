## 2) Super-clear bullet points (the "why it's phenomenal" list)

- **Scale-true blueprint.** Every cell is 25 cm — spacing mistakes happen on screen, not in soil.
- **One toggle to 3D.** The same plan stands up as a walkable voxel world — greenhouses, ponds, barns, animals.
- **Rehearse seasons.** Drought, heat, fertilizer, climate +2 °C — compare yield, water, carbon and profit before you commit a seed.
- **Real companion data.** Bad-neighbor crops get flagged while you draw; good pairings get halos.
- **Live weather.** Open-Meteo current + 7-day forecast, soil temperature and moisture, frost/heat/rain alerts.
- **USDA soil knowledge.** SSURGO lookup: texture, pH, organic matter, CEC, water-holding capacity.
- **A calendar that knows your frost.** Sow indoors → transplant → harvest windows, computed week by week.
- **50-crop library.** Growth models, water needs, spacing and yield estimates for what you actually grow.
- **Growth you can scrub.** Drag the date and crops move through six stages — plan the harvest, not just the planting.
- **Local-first.** Works offline, lives in your browser, exports PNG plans + a seed-shopping CSV. Nothing phones home.
- **Free & open source (MIT).** Beautiful tools for the people who feed us.

## 6) Don't overclaim (keep the launch honest)

- Simulations are **transparent what-if illustrations**, not an agronomic model — say "rehearse/compare," never "predicts."
- Frost dates are **climate normals**, user-overridable — "a starting point," never "guaranteed."
- 3D is a **planning twin** (procedural voxel charm), not satellite imagery or CAD.
- USDA soil works for **US locations** (falls back to SoilGrids name-only elsewhere).
- Weather needs **network**; everything else works offline.
