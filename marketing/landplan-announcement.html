■ One plan, two views

Blueprint first.
World on top.

The Plot Designer is a drafting tool: Select, Brush, Line, Rectangle, Fill, Pick, Asset, Erase, with 1×1, 3×3, and 5×5 brushes and undo all the way back. Eight starter templates — Salad Garden to Tent Starters — seed a plan in one click. Every cell is 25 cm, so a bed is as long as it actually is.

The overlays carry the agronomy: spacing violations outline in amber where a stand sits tighter than its catalog row allows, companion halos in green where the catalog endorses a pairing.

■ Built from the plan

The world is the plan, stood up in voxels.

Nothing in the 3D world is decorative filler. Crops grow through six stages from instanced voxel builders; hoophouses and greenhouses get their shells from the same plan records; a coop brings hens, a hive brings bees, a pond brings ducks.

Every builder is deterministic — same seed, identical rebuild — so the twin is an honest model of the plan, and the asset showcase prints the whole library like a specimen sheet.

■ Weather, rehearsal, ownership, advice

Weather is in the scene, not a sidebar footnote.

LandPlan pulls real forecasts from Open-Meteo and renders them into the world and the ops views. Frost, heat, rain, and dry alerts are derived from thresholds, and the thresholds are shown. Offline, the last good forecast stays and is labeled CACHED rather than quietly aging.

Rehearse the season before it costs you.

Simulate forks the plan and runs it: growth, water, stress, harvest windows. Runs are deterministic, so a run can be replayed and checked against itself. The Calendar is generated from frost dates, and the Dashboard answers what is ready this week.

Your data is yours, locally.

Farms persist in the browser. The plan exports as a PNG sheet with legend and scale bar, seed lists as CSV, the whole plan as JSON. A typed REST seam mirrors the README endpoint contract, so a backend can take over storage without changing a page.

Advice advises. You decide.

Jev is opt-in, per cell, and read-only by contract. If a live call fails, the scan falls back to local heuristics and is labeled as such. It reads the plan, the catalog, and the forecast; the verdicts carry a source and a confidence.

■ Known limits — stated plainly · 2026-09-20

No hosted backend
Storage, planning, simulation, and export all run in the browser, so there is no uptime or SLA claim to make. Hosted advice will require a server-side proxy; provider keys do not ship client-side.Src: README — backend swap via VITE_API_BASE_URL
Not built yet
Multi-user editing, rotation history across seasons, and live sensor integrations at scale. When one of these lands, it ships under a revision number, not a footnote.As of Rev 1.0 · 2026-09-20
